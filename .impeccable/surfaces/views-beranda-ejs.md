# Premium Sentra KI

Scope: beranda and shared navigation, typography, controls, footer; quieter refinements on the existing Operate/Read routes. Home mode: Persuade. Build path: code, as already selected by the owner. Navy/gold, official logos, factual data, custom icons and working forms remain binding.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An academic atlas of intellectual property, indexed by real inventions. Replace the equal-card marketing opener with large editorial type, a working catalogue search and an actual portfolio index.

OWN-WORLD: Deep institutional navy, restrained champagne gold, white reading surfaces. Bodoni Moda 400 for home display; Manrope for interface and data. Square-ended rules, lightly rounded controls, no decorative blur or neon glow.

STORY: Recognize Unisba, search its KI, inspect a featured record, continue to the directory or registration. Every number and record comes from existing server data.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width navy opening; two generous lines of display type; compact supporting copy; a long horizontal search and category shortcuts. A real-record index follows directly, with selectable inventor records rather than an ornamental image.

FORM: Grounded candidate 7, academic atlas; seed ead2c0b6. Alternatives considered: ceremonial folio, research monograph, partnership dossier, patent archive, exhibition register and institutional banner. Catalog ink simulation, drum machine, cloth simulation, Versailles, uniform-code and poster challengers declined on both audience identification and product clarity. Their disciplines raise bounded motion, clear active selection, responsive transformation, measured pacing, annotation clarity and type scale, without copying their motifs.

SIGNATURE: Lembar inovasi — a finite opening sequence and user-controlled portfolio leaf changes. Supporting feedback uses 150–250 ms transitions; the opening lasts at most 800 ms. No continuous scrolling effects, autoplay or new animation library. Reduced motion shows static content and instant state changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
